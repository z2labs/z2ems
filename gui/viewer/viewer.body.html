RF ports

Suggestions from the connectors, net names and net classes of the board. Ticked ones go into the simulation; nothing is forced.

Port numbers follow the list order; drag a port marker onto another pad to move it. Z0 is the reference impedance of the port (default 50 Ω).

Phase match

Channel paths pad to pad on the copper, delay with the εeff of each layer. absolute: before the calibration point, the phase at f0 must match. delay: calibrated at one frequency, the delay spread sets the phase error across the band.

Schematic: click the first pin, then the second pin on the same net.

Result

|J| of the port-1 excitation, dB relative to the strongest current on any layer (black: −40 dB or less). Hide layers to look at the inner planes.
